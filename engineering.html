Ancient Maritime Innovations

1. Port & Harbor Structures

Marine archaeological surveys off the coast of Gujarat reveal submerged stone masonry designed to stabilize the ancient port against strong intertidal currents.

  • Intertidal Retaining Walls: Large, dressed limestone blocks fitted together to absorb tidal wave impacts.
  • Mooring Piers: Built with deep stone anchors to secure incoming trading vessels from the Arabian Sea.

2. Corrosion-Resistant Alloys

Artifacts retrieved from the Gomti River mouth showcase early mastery over metallurgical composition suited for saltwater marine environments.

  • Copper-Bronze Alloys: Seals, bronze hooks, and tools crafted with high purity to prevent rust.
  • Iron Anchor Seals: Specialized iron fittings utilized in heavy stone anchors.

3. Urban Grid & Sea Gates

According to ancient texts and modern topographical mappings, the city was organized into distinct functional sectors protected by sea fortresses.

  • Integrated drainage systems leading into coastal channels.
  • Fortified gateway entrances functioning as naval checkpoints.
